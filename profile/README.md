# George Merrick Troop 7 of Coral Gables, Inc.

George Merrick Troop 7 of Coral Gables, Inc. is the Florida 501(c)(3)
not-for-profit corporation that holds the deed to the historic Troop 7 Log
Cabin (the "Scout Hut") at 1107 South Greenway Drive, Coral Gables, FL 33134.
The corporation administers the long-term lease with the City of Coral
Gables and pays the property's utilities, so that the Scouting units who meet
there (Troop 7, Pack 7, Crew 7, and Troop 719) can keep using it.

Incorporated March 9, 1967 (Florida Document Number 712382).

Learn more, including our history, board of directors, and how to reach us,
at [gmt7.org](https://gmt7.org).
